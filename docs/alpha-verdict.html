Is α a tuning knob?

No — and not because we failed to measure an effect. The Rényi order α reaches the optimizer through exactly one integer, and in the non-DP regime that integer is 1 for every α ≥ 1. The whole half-line is one algorithm. Every α "effect" this project ever reported is GPU nondeterminism.

§0Verdict

Four claims, and what each rests on

  1. 01 α is unidentifiable, not merely inert. α enters only via ⌊Nα⌋. Certified from one logged scalar in 36/36 runs (Thm 2), and confirmed exactly by realised depth: ≤ 1.9 % of matrix-steps differ, by ≤ 1 slot. Proved
  2. 02 Every reported α effect is nondeterminism. Maximum effect α can mediate: 1.1e-6 – 2.8e-5. Replicate floor at that depth: 3.0e-4. Observed differences run 6× – 529× larger than α could possibly cause. Refuted
  3. 03 The "per-matrix adaptive depth" was never adaptive. At α ≥ 1 the rule assigns the same depth to all 196 matrices at all 260 rotations. "Heterogeneity contributes nothing" is true because no heterogeneity occurred. Retired
  4. 04 No entropy order can work — the statistic is scale-blind. Nα is invariant to the momentum's scale, so it cannot see signal-to-noise. It responds to added noise with the wrong sign. This is the theory contribution. Proved

Operationally nothing changes: Shannon (α = 1) sits inside the equivalence class. What changes is what the paper claims. Ship the constant the rule degenerates to, and ship the impossibility theorem as the result.

§1The map

§2Thm 1

α collapses onto one integer

LoRA-XS trains only the tiny core R ∈ ℝr×r inside a frozen B R A. LoRA-XSe re-draws part of that frozen basis every few steps. Which part is decided from the momentum spectrum:

pi = σi²Σj σj²   ·   Hα(p) = 11 − α log Σi piα   ·   Nα = eHα
k = ⌊Nα(p)⌋ + m  ·  re = clip(r − k, 1, r−1)  ·  rkeep = r − re

Keep the top rkeep singular directions of the momentum; resample the other re from the orthogonal complement. A grep for the α variable in xse.py returns exactly three live uses: compute H, compute re, and log it. That is the entire attack surface of α.

Theorem 1 · exact equivalence

The trajectory depends on (α, m) only through the integer field k(ℓ)t = ⌊Nα(p(ℓ)t)⌋ + m. So if two settings satisfy ⌊Nα⌋ + m = ⌊Nα′⌋ + m′ for every matrix ℓ and every rotation t along the coupled trajectory, the two runs are the same stochastic algorithm: identical trajectory law, identical realisations under a shared random stream.

Proof — Induction on t. States coincide at t = 0. Given identical state at rotation t, the momenta coincide, hence p, hence k, hence rkeep and re. The update is a deterministic function of (state, rkeep, random draw): the kept slices are taken at index rkeep, the explore block is a draw of re directions from the complement, and R is re-expressed by the 2×2 block projection. Same state + same integer + same draw ⇒ same next state. ∎

Three corollaries that each kill a line of enquiry

  • Identifiability. Nα ∈ [1, r] and is non-increasing in α, so α ↦ ⌊Nα⌋ is a step function with at most r−1 jumps. α has at most r distinguishable values no matter how finely you sweep it. It is not a continuous hyperparameter; it is a selector among ≤ r integers.
  • The margin is an exact offset. k = ⌊Nα⌋ + m is additively separable, so re(α,m) − re(α′,m) is independent of m. A margin sweep can never break an α-degeneracy — which is why the m = 0 test did not decollapse α, and why m and α proved interchangeable.
  • The old α comparison was a replicate set. The three depth-14 runs used as the project's α test execute the same algorithm. Their 5.7e-4 spread is the noise floor, not an effect that later "vanished". Theorem 1 is the licence the earlier analysis was missing — it is what makes those runs a valid floor rather than a circular one.
§3Thm 2

Certified from a single logged scalar

Let δ = 1 − p1 be the energy leaking out of the dominant momentum direction. Then the collapse is checkable a priori, without running anything.

Theorem 2 · collapse certificates

(a) α ≥ 2. For α > 1, Σi piα ≥ p1α and x ↦ x1/(1−α) is decreasing, so Nα ≤ p1−α/(α−1) ≤ p1−2 for all α ≥ 2. Hence

p1 > 2−1/2 ≈ 0.7071  ⟹  ⌊Nα⌋ = 1  for all α ≥ 2.

(b) α = 1. The grouping identity H1(p) = Hb(δ) + δ·H(p̃) with H(p̃) ≤ log(r−1) gives exp(Hb(δ) + δ log(r−1)) < 2 ⟹ ⌊N1⌋ = 1.

(c) α ∈ (0,1). Hölder on the tail: Nα ≤ [(1−δ)α + (r−1)1−αδα]1/(1−α), with equality iff the tail is uniform. This defines the threshold α*(δ, r) below which α becomes live.

(d) Exact, no bound. By monotonicity in α: if Nβ < 2 for any β, then ⌊Nα⌋ = 1 for all α ≥ β.

The certificate on our data. p1 = 1/N∞, and both are already logged every rotation.

Theorem 3 · first-order mediation bound

Under (A1) — the loss responds to the depth schedule to first order, with sensitivity measured by uniform depth perturbations — |L(α) − L(α′)| ≲ Λ · D. With Λ = 3.52e-5 per slot and all contrasts living at depth ≥ 11.3: the largest effect α can mediate anywhere in the sweep is 2.8e-5, against a 3.0e-4 replicate floor. α is not "flat" — it is below the resolution of the apparatus by more than an order of magnitude, and buying seeds cannot change that.

(A1) is an assumption — it is the honest pairing, since a uniform 1-slot shift is dose 1, and it is supported by the matched-depth residual audits (7 arms, |residual| ≤ 1.9e-4, random sign). The dose–response test in the figure needs no assumption at all: whatever generates those differences, it is not α.

§5Thm 4

The no-go: an entropy rank cannot see signal-to-noise

This is the part that outlives the paper. The question "is direction i signal or noise?" is intrinsically about σi relative to the noise scale. A scale-invariant statistic cannot express it — and Nα is computed on a normalised spectrum, so it is scale-invariant for every α.

Theorem 4 · scale-blindness, and the wrong sign

(i) Nα(p(cM)) = Nα(p(M)) for every c > 0 and every α.

(ii) In the spiked model M = Σj≤s θjujvjT + ς r−1/2G, the BBP transition makes spike j detectable iff θj > ς, so the oracle keep-set K*= {j : θj > ς} has |K*| non-increasing in ς.

(iii) With the mean-field spectrum σi² = θi² + ς², every ratio pi/pj = (θi²+ς²)/(θj²+ς²) compresses toward 1 as ς grows, so p(ς) is majorised by p(ς′) for ς > ς′. Hα is Schur-concave for every α ≥ 0, hence Nα is non-decreasing in ς, with Nα → r.

(iv) Therefore re = r − ⌊Nα⌋ − m is non-increasing in noise while the correct r − |K*| is non-decreasing. The signs are opposite, for every α. No order repairs this: the defect is in the functional, not the order — which is why the α programme was doomed before the first run.

What the margin was actually doing. In k = ⌊Nα⌋ + m, m is the only term that can encode an SNR correction, since ⌊Nα⌋ cannot. That is why m was as load-bearing as α, and why the two proved interchangeable. The paper should stop calling m a "safety buffer" and call it what it is: the rule's only noise-aware term, an uncalibrated constant standing in for a threshold.

§6Paper

What to write

Keep — this is still the result

Rotating the frozen LoRA-XS basis recovers full-LoRA quality at 201× fewer trainable parameters, ε-free by post-processing. Multi-seed, paired, at both ε = 1 and ε = 3. Unaffected by anything here.

Keep — the mechanism, quoted against the right floor

The depth curve: −3.46e-3 from depth 1 → 13, monotone-saturating, slope decaying 19×. The rule: refresh at least ~55 % of the rank; above that it is one flat plateau. Going shallow is the only mistake, and it costs a quarter of the entire rotation benefit — 11× the depth-matched floor of 3.0e-4.

Reframe — ship the constant, not the knob

re = r − 1 − m

"Keep the dominant momentum direction, refresh the rest." Then prove (Thm 2a, 36/36 runs certified) that this is what every α ≥ 2 realises in this regime, and (Thm 2b) what Shannon realises too at the depths we use. The Rényi machinery becomes what it actually is: the diagnostic that establishes rank-1 dominance — pooled p1 = 0.847, N1 = 1.55; in the depth-14 family p1 = 0.900 with the top two directions carrying 98.8 % of momentum energy — and therefore derives the constant. Correct by construction rather than tuned: a stronger claim than a tuned α, and one no reviewer can ask you to sweep.

New contribution — the negative, stated as theory

Theorems 1–4 are self-contained: spectral-entropy effective-rank statistics cannot implement capacity selection, for two independent reasons — quantization destroys identifiability (≤ r distinguishable settings; here exactly one), and scale-invariance gives the wrong sign of response to noise. This is not a story about our optimizer. Entropy and effective-rank statistics on normalised spectra are used for rank allocation across the PEFT literature, and both objections apply verbatim. That is the section a reviewer will remember.

Retire explicitly

  • α as a tuning knob.
  • Per-matrix adaptive depth as a benefit — there was no per-matrix variation to benefit from.
  • The margin as a "safety buffer".

And do not repeat these two errors — they are in the doc history

A noise floor taken from a contrast between configurations (circular: it assumes α has no effect in order to conclude α has no effect), and a floor taken from a different depth or family than the comparison. Theorem 1 is what licenses the depth-14 group as a genuine replicate set — the argument the earlier passes were missing.

§7GPU

Three runs worth buying

The α verdict needs nothing. These close real gaps.

#1 — worth it · 3 runs · decouple depth from adaptivity in the noise floor

med-nodp-fixed-re13 (non-adaptive, p_e = 0.8125, depth 13) × seeds {42, 43, 44}. Right now every floor in the paper is confounded: all deep runs are adaptive, all shallow ones are not. If the triple lands at ~3e-4, the floor is a depth effect and "adaptive runs are 20× noisier" is withdrawn. If it lands at ~3e-5, adaptivity genuinely costs variance and that is a reportable cost. Either answer is load-bearing for every effect size you quote. This is the one I would actually run.

#2 — worth it if the theorem should be general · 4 runs · escape the null space

Non-DP, r = 64, α ∈ {0.5, 1, 2, ∞}, m = 2, seed 42. Theorem 2 says the collapse is a property of (r, p1), not of α — and the DP data already shows the statistic escaping the floor as r grows (⌊N1⌋ = 1 at r = 2, 4 → 4 at r = 16 under ε = 3). Prediction: at r = 64 non-DP, ⌊Nα⌋ separates across α and genuine per-matrix heterogeneity appears for the first time. Then either loss is still flat — the strongest possible negative, "α identifiable and still inert" — or it is not, and you have found the regime where α matters. Both are publishable. The only experiment that can still surprise you.

#3 — free, do it anyway · 0 runs

Log minℓ p1 and maxℓ N0.5 per rotation (one line near xse.py:466). That makes the Theorem 2 certificate checkable per matrix instead of on a layer mean. It rides along on #1 and #2.

Do not run

More α sweeps at r = 16 non-DP (unidentifiable, 36/36 certified). More margin sweeps (exact offset — cannot decollapse α). More seeds at depth 14 (they are replicates: useful for the floor, worthless for α). The two seedrep-*-s44 runs in flight are fine to let finish — they take the replicate group to n = 6 and sharpen the floor. They cannot reopen α.

If you want a genuinely adaptive rule — and a positive result

The replacement must be scale-aware, or Theorem 4 kills it on arrival.

candidatefixes Thm 1?fixes Thm 4?status
Noise-thresholded count #{σi > c·ς̂} — BBP / Donoho–Gavish yesyes already implemented (XSE_MP_SHRINKAGE) and already logged every run as xs_spread/rec_rank_*
Energy threshold min{k : Σi≤k pi ≥ τ} partlyno — still normalised not a fix; only a smoother knob

The thresholded count is the right object: it responds to noise with the correct sign, and under DP the noise scale ς is known exactly (multiplier × clipping norm / batch, already logged), so the rule has no free parameter at all — strictly stronger than "we tuned α".

But be honest about where it can be validated. In non-DP the loss is flat over depth 9–15 within the floor, and every sensible rule lands in that band — the spike count puts depth at ≈ 8, the plateau edge. Non-DP is structurally incapable of discriminating between depth-selection rules. A positive adaptive result has to come from a regime with curvature in the depth response: DP, larger r, or a tighter step budget. State that as a design constraint up front, not a failure to apologise for.
